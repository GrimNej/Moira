# Demo video script

For whoever records the video: the 3-minute script (blueprint §15.1).

## Script

Hard limit 3:00; the script runs 2:50. Capture and save-time research come before the first
question, so nobody mistakes Moira for "chat with your notes". Sample memory only, plus live
captures of real public posts. Captions burned in; anything sped up is labeled "2× speed".

| Time | Scene | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:12 | Hook | A cluttered notes app (sample) scrolling: links, half-notes, an email | "I save everything. I find nothing. Notes need names; search needs exact words; and a link with no note is gone." |
| 0:12–0:32 | Capture | Phone: share an Instagram reel to the bot → 👌; type three FPS fixes → 👌; voice note → 👌; screenshot of an email signature → 👌 | "Moira is a chat. I send it anything and forget about it. No folders, no titles, no questions." |

It understands the clues, searches by time, source and meaning, and flies me to the exact star. That reel had no note; it was researched the moment I saved it." |

## Recording notes

- Record the live captures with the production bot and the owner's phone on Wi-Fi; the answers
  in the video are real, not mocked. Use real public posts (for example Meta's official
  accounts) so live research genuinely works on camera.
- Desktop: OBS Studio at 1080p. Phone: the built-in screen recorder. Edit in DaVinci Resolve or
  Shotcut. Quiet royalty-free music.
- Before recording: a fresh sandbox, caps at judging values, notifications off, no tokens or
  personal data on screen.
- Fill the two placeholders in the Impact scene from `docs/eval/RESULTS.md` (the real-model row)
  and the tester count in DEVLOG.
